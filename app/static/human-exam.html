What MumGuard evaluates

The final finding is built from multiple evidence channels rather than a single colour or hotspot.

Focused thermal response

Looks for localized/core hyperthermia-like patterns inside the reconstructed side.

LEFT vs RIGHT asymmetry

Compares the two reconstructed sides and measures bilateral differences.

Thermal distribution

Measures how the relative thermal signal is distributed across the visible TLC response.

Local visual AI evidence

Adds local morphology/novelty evidence to complement the thermal measurement layer.

Possible results: Tumor-like pattern detected, No tumor-like pattern detected, or Inconclusive when the examination does not support a reliable binary research result.
The Research Concern Score summarizes current research evidence. It is not a calibrated cancer probability or a clinical diagnosis.
