Subject: Research data request — contact liquid crystal thermography AI study

Dear Dr [Name],

I am working on a research prototype for analysis of **contact / liquid-crystal thermography (LCT)** images. We are specifically trying to avoid transferring conclusions from infrared thermography into a different acquisition modality, so we are looking for de-identified, case-linked contact-LCT data with a reliable reference standard.

Your work on [study/project title] appears directly relevant. If data sharing is possible, we would be grateful to discuss access to de-identified original RGB contact-LCT thermograms together with the minimum metadata needed to keep evaluation scientifically valid:

- stable de-identified subject/case ID and image-to-subject mapping;
- malignant / benign / control outcome and the reference-standard provenance for each case;
- TLC foil/formulation or temperature range, where known;
- device/camera and acquisition protocol, including view/position and timepoint where applicable;
- explicit terms for research use, model development, and any restrictions on future commercial prototype use.

We do **not** need names, medical-record numbers, contact details, or other direct patient identifiers. Multiple images from the same subject would remain grouped during model evaluation to prevent train/test leakage.

Our present system is a research image-analysis prototype and is not being represented as a validated diagnostic device. We would also be happy to keep any shared dataset private and follow an institutional data-use agreement if required.

If the raw data cannot be shared, even a smaller de-identified subset or guidance on the appropriate institutional/archive contact would be extremely helpful.

Thank you for considering the request.

Best regards,
Omar Khair
